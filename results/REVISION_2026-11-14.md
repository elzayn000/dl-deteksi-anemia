# Results refresh — 14 November 2026

The refreshed repository package contains the current source CSVs, manuscript Tables I–XI, cleaning audit JSON files, Supplementary Tables S1–S3, seven SVG confusion matrices, two SVG CNN training curves, and ten notebooks (16–25).

The final experiment dataset contains 1,563 images (901 anemic, 662 non-anemic): Palm 520, Nail 528, and Conjunctiva 515. An intermediate cleaned collection contained 1,567 images before four Nail images were excluded during the final leakage audit.

Fairness analysis retains ITA and within-site L* tercile groups; the legacy 8-bit L* grouping is excluded. Ablation testing reports Nadeau–Bengio corrected p-values and Holm adjustment for the family of 11 AUC contrasts. Raw pediatric image data and four large internal audit/feature CSVs are not included in the compact package.
