# Results refresh — 14 November 2026

This PR synchronizes the repository overview and dataset guide, corrects the main ablation/fairness source exports, adds manuscript Tables I–XI as individual CSV files, refreshes Supplementary Tables S1–S3, and commits the SVG archive containing seven confusion matrices and two CNN training curves.

The final experiment dataset contains 1,563 images (901 anemic, 662 non-anemic): Palm 520, Nail 528, and Conjunctiva 515. An intermediate cleaned collection contained 1,567 images before four Nail images were excluded during the final leakage audit.

Fairness analysis retains ITA and within-site L* tercile groups; the legacy 8-bit L* grouping is excluded. Ablation testing reports Nadeau–Bengio corrected p-values and Holm adjustment for the family of 11 AUC contrasts. Raw pediatric image data and four large internal audit/feature CSVs are not included.
